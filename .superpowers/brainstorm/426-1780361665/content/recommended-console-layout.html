Why this structure

Strengths

  • Reports, run records, and launching evaluations are separate enough to stay readable.
  • Long-running job progress is never hidden behind a tab.
  • Datasets remain available, but do not dominate the main reporting workflow.

Trade-off

  • The page becomes a console, so we should keep styling restrained and dense.
  • Mobile should collapse the job rail below the active section.
